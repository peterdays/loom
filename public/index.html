Projects as a woven graph. Colour is time: the oldest work burns violet at the edge, the newest runs gold toward the bright core of now. Rings mark how long each body of work lived.

Colour is time

  • Cooled — ended. Its light stops at the latest addition.
  • Glowing — ongoing. Its light reaches the core of now.
